Behind the glass

The thinking happens on the machine, the same way it does at home. The cloud only carries your record between your devices; it doesn’t decide anything.

1

Recognize

2

To make it real

Beyond the current spec

Today a profile lives on a machine through a slot the machine’s administrator reserves. A café can’t do that for every passer-by. This dream needs one new idea:

  • A visit pass. A short-lived permission the user grants from the app. It lets a venue’s machine use the profile and the caffeine record for one visit. It uses no slot and needs no invite.
  • Venue opt-in. The café’s or office’s administrator turns visits on for their machine.
  • Nothing left behind. When the visit ends, the machine drops its copy. The only trace is the brew, which goes into Diane’s own record.
  • Identifying yourself. Tap the phone, or enter the profile PIN, as on an Office device today.

Illustrative figures. Diane’s settings come from the prototype’s example profile: Wake-Hours Ceiling 490 mg, Bedtime Ceiling 75 mg, Caffeine Decay Rate 4.5 hr, bedtime 11:30 PM. The Culminara Caffè machine and the Caffevolve Passport are concepts, not part of the current design.
