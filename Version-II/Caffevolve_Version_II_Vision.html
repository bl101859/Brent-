Caffevolve™  ·  Vision for Version II

One profile.
Every device.

Take your Caffevolve with you, and shape it on your phone.

Caffevolve already tunes every cup to the person drinking it. Version II takes that personal touch out of the kitchen and connects home, office and café into a single Caffevolve universe.

The big change

Take your Caffevolve with you

Your profile and your active standing go wherever you go. Walk up to any Caffevolve device that has opened its door to Passport, at a café, an office or a friend’s home, and pick up right where you left off when you walked out of the house.

You get the full control you have in your own kitchen, an extension of home limited only by what that device carries.

Today, away from a Caffevolve device, a drink can only be trimmed by size or shot count.

The second change

Shape it on your phone

Complete most of your profile, your Sub-Profiles and your plans on your phone, wherever is comfortable, with no small-screen fiddling at the device.

Your device is where you brew. Your phone is where you shape it.

Also comingPay from your phone and skip the queue.

How the universe grows

Office

Colleagues meet Caffevolve at work. Some of them bring one home.

Home & friends

Guests at a friend’s home brew their own cup and see what it can do.

Café

Home and office customers walking in show the owner there is demand for the device.

More devices

Once installed, a café device meets potentially hundreds of new people.

Every device makes every Passport more valuable, and every Passport makes the next device easier to justify.
