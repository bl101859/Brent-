The Caffeine Management & Delivery Platform

Coffee Without
a Curfew.

Caffevolve learns how each person responds to caffeine, works out the right caffeine content for every cup of the day, and then brews exactly that, at home, at work and at the café. Not less coffee. More of the day to enjoy it.

The problem

Every coffee drinker carries two personal limits: past one, the lift turns to jitters; past the other, caffeine still in the system costs the night’s sleep. With no way to see either, people guess, and most simply stop by mid-afternoon. Decaf keeps the ritual but not the lift.

The answer

Change what is in the cup, not whether you have it. Caffevolve blends caffeinated and decaf beans in the exact ratio each moment allows. The cup is no longer the decision. The blend inside it is.

One system · three components
Each one is incomplete alone.
01 · The brain

Platform Intelligence

Keeps a live estimate of the caffeine active in each person’s system and computes a personal blend recommendation for every cup.

02 · Where it becomes coffee

Precision Brewing Device

Dispenses caffeinated and decaf beans in the recommended ratio, blends them before grinding, and brews to SCA Golden Cup standards.

03 · Out the door

CaffevolveGo

The companion app that logs cups had elsewhere and recommends drinks at the office, café or store that fit the person’s limits.

What no one else has built

Every feature below runs in the working prototype
01

Caffeine Behavior Instrument

Plain, everyday questions (how many drinks before you feel wired, the latest caffeine that still lets you sleep) become a personal Wake Hours Ceiling, Bedtime Ceiling and Caffeine Decay Rate. No lab test, no wearable.

Prototype · su8Compute()
02

Dual-ceiling blend engine

Each cup is tested against both limits at once: will it overstimulate right now, and what will still be active at bedtime? The strongest blend that clears both becomes the recommendation.

Prototype · computeRecommendedBlend()
03

A live 24-hour caffeine standing

Every logged cup, home or away, decays at the person’s own rate, and leftover caffeine carries past midnight into the next day. Recommendations always start from the whole picture.

Prototype · eceDecayedMg(), getHalfLifeMins(), midnight carry-forward
04

Blend-then-grind brewing

Two hoppers, one caffeinated and one decaf, feed a single grinder so caffeine is evenly distributed. Flavor and wellness additives are released into the coffee stream. Filed as patent-pending apparatus claims.

Prototype · hopperConfig (A caffeinated, B decaf)
05

Offsite Advisory System

In front of a menu, the user picks worksite, coffee shop or convenience store. CaffevolveGo shows the full venue menu and ranks first the drinks that fit, before the order is placed.

Prototype · oasCompute(), oasRenderDrinks()
06

Plan my Day

Lay out the whole day and see its peak and bedtime level before the first cup. Protect the one drink you won’t give up, and one tap suggests the smallest changes that bring the day back under. A saved plan resurfaces at each brew.

Prototype · pmdProject(), pmdComputeFix(), pmdReminderMatch()
07

Sub-Profiles

Up to six day-and-time windows on the premium tier (three on others) that set strength and additives automatically. About five minutes to set up once, then a few seconds to start every brew.

Prototype · getActiveSubProfile(), MAX_SUB_PROFILES
08

An advisor, never a gatekeeper

Any recommendation can be accepted, adjusted or overridden, and the device brews whatever the user chooses. The platform recommends; the person decides.

Prototype · manual blend override (brewSession.isManual)
