The Problem

The slumber that clears. The mind that arrives. Every day, coffee creates the conditions for our best work and our most human moments. But that lift follows a curve, not a straight line. Beyond a personal threshold the gains reverse: agitation replaces calm, and long after the cup is finished, the same compound that sharpened the afternoon quietly steals the night.

The Reaction

So most of us stop. Not because the work is done, but because too many restless nights taught us that another cup after a certain point is a gamble. What we give up is real: the meeting that needed one more hour of sharp thinking, the afternoon that could have finished strong instead of fading quietly away.

The Caffevolve Way

Every coffee drinker has a personal caffeine threshold, a limit set by their own body before caffeine starts working against them. Each morning, that threshold becomes a daily allowance: a budget you draw from, cup by cup. Each standard cup claims a fixed slice, so the choice has always been binary. “Yes, I’ll have another cup.” Or, “No, I really shouldn’t.” Period.

Caffevolve draws on that same budget with far more precision. It calculates the right caffeine dose for every cup, stretching the same threshold across more hours of the day. Not fewer cups. More cups, each one right-sized.

What no one else has built

Every feature below runs in the working prototype
01

Caffeine Behavior Instrument

Purpose-built questions, simple to answer yet precise in what they measure, map each person’s caffeine comfort zone: their personal caffeine thresholds and estimated caffeine metabolism rate. No lab test, no wearable.

02

Dual-threshold blend engine

Every caffeine blend recommendation is tested against both personal thresholds at once: will it overstimulate right now, and how much will still be active at bedtime? The platform then recommends the strongest blend that clears both, so you get all the caffeine the moment allows without crossing either line.

03

Always know where you stand

Before every cup, you know exactly how much caffeine is still at work in your system and how much room you have left in the day. Every cup counts, home or away, so the next one brewed gets a precise caffeine blend recommendation, never a guess.

04

Precision Dispense, Blend, Grind & Brew

Two bean hoppers dispense caffeinated and decaf beans by weight into a dedicated Blend Chamber, which mixes them evenly before they are ground in the burr grinder and brewed. Flavor and wellness additives are released into the brew on its way to the cup or carafe. Filed as patent-pending apparatus claims.

05

Offsite Advisory System

With your real-time caffeine standing on the app, choose your location type (worksite, café or convenience store) and the Offsite Advisory System presents a typical beverage menu for that kind of venue, ranking first the drinks that fit within your thresholds. No calculating: it’s informed choice when it matters, with the same clarity away from home you’d have standing at your brewer.

06

Plan my Day

Lay out the whole day in advance: every drink and its caffeine content, morning to night, in one view. Ease off the morning to make room for a cup deep in the afternoon or evening, and watch the whole day settle inside your personal caffeine thresholds before you drink a drop. Then the plan reminds you each time you reach for a drink.

How it is different

Everything else remembers. Caffevolve reasons, and acts.

Every connected coffee machine on the market does the same fundamental thing: it remembers and it brews. Jura remembers your cappuccino, De’Longhi recalls your saved profile, and the app starts the brew from your phone. That’s real convenience, but memory only gives back what you told it, and storing what you like says nothing about whether you should have it now.

Caffevolve reasons. It learns how you respond to caffeine, weighs that against everything already active in your system, works out what this specific cup should be, and then brews exactly that.

Memory and reasoning are different architectures. A competitor can add another saved profile or app button and still have a machine that only remembers. No existing product pairs a device, an app, and a reasoning layer to deliver a precise caffeine-managed cup of delicious coffee.
