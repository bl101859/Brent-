Bean families
Arabica & Robusta
Approximately 60% of coffee produced worldwide is arabica and some 40% is robusta. Using the same family in both hoppers keeps your blend tasting seamlessly consistent at every caffeine ratio.
Arabica
Specialty gold standard
0.8–1.4% caffeine
Because of its delicacy, it is ideally roasted light to medium to preserve nuanced fruity and floral characteristics. Primarily used for pour-overs, drip coffee, and standalone espresso shots.
Robusta
Bold booster
1.7–4.0% caffeine
Because it lacks delicate acidity but brings a thick body and crema, it excels with medium-to-dark roasts. Widely used in instant coffees and traditional Italian espresso blends for a heavier, richer character.
Roast levels
Light · Medium · Dark
Roast level shapes the flavor character of your coffee. Matching the roast level across both hoppers ensures your blend tastes balanced and consistent regardless of caffeine ratio.
Light roast
Roasted to preserve the bean's natural character. Expect bright acidity, delicate fruit and floral notes, and a lighter body. Higher in perceived caffeine by weight. Best suited for pour-overs and drip brewing.
Medium roast
The most versatile roast. Balanced acidity, sweetness, and body with caramel and nutty undertones. Works well across all brew methods and blends seamlessly with most additives.
Dark roast
Bold and full-bodied with low acidity. The roast character dominates — think bittersweet chocolate, smoke, and deep richness. Lower perceived caffeine by weight. Classic for espresso and French press.

ABOUT YOUR BREW
Your Caffeine Comfort Zone
How Caffevolve™ personalizes your blend
Caffevolve™ builds a personal Caffeine Comfort Zone for each user — four parameters calibrated to how caffeine actually behaves in your body. Every blend recommendation is calculated against these values, not a generic setting.
Wake-Hours Ceiling
The total residual caffeine level at which caffeine stops helping and starts causing jitteriness or overstimulation. Every daytime blend is kept below this wall. Starting range: 200–400 mg depending on your answers.
Bedtime Ceiling
The residual caffeine level above which sleep quality may be affected. Every recommendation is checked against this too — if a caffeinated blend would carry too much into the night, the system says so. Starting range: 30–70 mg.
Peak-Zone Floor
The minimum active caffeine level where you feel focused and alert. Recommendations stay above this floor when possible — below it, the blend isn't doing much for you. Typical range: 40–60 mg.
Caffeine Decay Rate
How quickly your body clears caffeine — measured as the time for your residual level to halve. A faster clearer (shorter half-life) can drink later and still sleep well. Typical range: 3–8 hours.
Guest profiles use a simplified starting ceiling (High · 200 mg / Moderate · 300 mg / Low · 400 mg). These are conservative priors — a registered profile runs the full calibration instrument to compute all four values from your actual habits.
