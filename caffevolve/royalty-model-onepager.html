Model inputs

  • Café engine (v3, assumed — pilot-measured): N = 52 setups/café/yr · c_cafe = 15% · r1 = 0.5. Per placement: 3.9 units yr 1, 7.8/yr sustained.
  • Office engine (v2, assumed — stock-and-flow): S = 32 · c_stock = 17.5% · W = 2 yrs · turnover 13% · c_flow = 10%. Per placement: 2.8, 2.8, then 0.42/yr.
  • Direct / home (assumed — independent, DTC-measured): Year-1 750 units, 35%/yr growth, less a 15% overlap haircut (buyers with a commercial touchpoint, removed to avoid double-counting Seeded). Decoupled from the ramp — a floor even at Low.
  • Commercial ramp (anchored — arena-split, Base): office 4, 70, 520, 2200, 5500 and café 2, 50, 380, 1600, 4000 units/yr; sum = 6 → 9,500/yr. Middle years & split illustrative pending the OEM-signed ramp.
  • Locked economics: net wholesale ≈ 50% MSRP ($1,250 / $300 / $100) · 5 / 7 / 8 royalty band at base 7% · Intro/Premier mix 70 / 30.
