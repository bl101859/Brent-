Model inputs

  • Café engine (v3, assumed — pilot-measured): N = 52 setups/café/yr · c_cafe = 15% · r1 = 0.5. Per placement: 3.9 units yr 1, 7.8/yr sustained.
  • Office engine (v2, assumed — stock-and-flow): S = 32 · c_stock = 17.5% · W = 2 yrs · turnover 13% · c_flow = 10%. Per placement: 2.8, 2.8, then 0.42/yr.
  • Direct / home (optional — OFF in base case): least-anchored input, excluded from the headline. Zero until the first licensee ship (Year 2 earliest), then 750 units growing 15%/yr, net of a 15% overlap haircut. Upside only, gated on a DTC test.
  • Commercial ramp (anchored — OEM-signed): Base = 2 OEMs by Yr 5, total 6, 250, 1500, 4500, 9500 units/yr, split office/café by OFRAC (office 3, 180, 1001, 2799, 5501; café 3, 70, 499, 1701, 3999). Low/High = 1 / 4 OEMs. Editable — replace with your live signings.
  • Locked economics: net wholesale ≈ 50% MSRP ($1,250 / $300 / $100) · 5 / 7 / 8 royalty band at base 7% · Intro/Premier mix 60 / 40.

Ceiling check (top-down guardrail)

Year-5 total units — 9,500 commercial + 21,812 seeded consumer = 31,312 — against a premium appliance shelf of 1,300,000 units/yr = 2.4% of shelf — a credible low-single-digit slice. Shelf is a derived figure (see Sources).
