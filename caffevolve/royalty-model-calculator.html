Assumed vs anchored

Anchored · research ramp

  • Commercial units placed, Yr 1–5, arena-split office/café, Low/Base/High by OEM signings.

Assumed · pilot-measured

  • Five office levers — size, stock conversion, window, turnover, new-hire conversion.
  • Three café levers — N, c_cafe, r1 (was "assumed zero" in v2; v3 adds the model).
  • Direct home demand — d₀, g — optional, off by default, least-anchored — not in the base case; upside only, and only after first licensee ship (net of overlap haircut h).
  • Intro / Premier consumer mix.

Locked

  • MSRP tiers · net-wholesale ≈ 50% ($1,250 / $300 / $100).
  • 5 / 7 / 8 royalty band at base 7%.

Mix, not rate, is the largest driver of consumer royalty. Attach rate is folded into placements — under field-of-use exclusivity attach is ≈100% within the lane, so "units placed" is the attached-unit count.

Directional model, not a forecast. Consumer volume is seeding-dominated and conditional; the forecast is sensitive to N (café throughput), the least-anchored input, which the café pilot anchors. One of the 1–2 measured pilots should be a café placement, not only an office.
